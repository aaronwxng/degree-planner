Completed uses earned credit only. Projected includes planned and in-progress courses. Subject-based checks assume courses have no substantial math/CS content; use the course editor to exclude exceptions. Course existence and all cross-listings are not verified.

Breadth Each course fills at most one category

Depth

1.50 units in one subject, including 0.50 units at 300-level or above; or a three-course prerequisite chain totalling at least 1.50 units.

Breadth courses may also count toward depth. List I’s humanities exclusion applies only to breadth. Automatic chain checks currently cover reviewed JAPAN 101R/111R → 102R → 201R paths; other chains need review.

Communication

Two distinct courses: one from List I with at least 60%, plus another from List I or II.

See eligible communication courses

First List I course should be on campus and must be completed before 2A. Mode of delivery and transfer-credit approvals need separate review.

Core & upper-year course requirements
